# Power BI Production DAX Measures Repository

## 1. Overview & Setup Architecture

This repository contains the complete, enterprise-grade DAX measures designed for the **E-Commerce Sales & Customer Intelligence Analytics Platform**.

### Enterprise Design Standards
1. **Dedicated Measures Table**: All measures reside in a decoupled table `_Measures` to simplify schema navigation.
2. **Display Folders**: Measures are categorized into 4 numbered folders:
   - `01 Financial KPIs`
   - `02 Volume & Operational KPIs`
   - `03 Customer Intelligence`
   - `04 Time Intelligence`
3. **Strict Delivered Filter**: In accordance with business rules, sales revenue, freight, gross profit, and review scores filter strictly for delivered transactions (`dim_orders[order_status] = "delivered"`), unless analyzing cancellations.
4. **Currency & Unit Formatting**:
   - Currency: `R$ #,##0.00; -R$ #,##0.00; "R$ 0.00"` (Strict Brazilian Reais BRL)
   - Percentages: `0.00%`
   - Counts & Volumes: `#,##0`

## 2. DAX Measures by Logical Folder

### Folder 01: Financial KPIs

#### 1. Total Delivered Merchandise Revenue
```dax
Total Delivered Merchandise Revenue = 
CALCULATE(
    SUM(fact_order_items[price]),
    dim_orders[order_status] = "delivered"
)
```
- **Folder**: `01 Financial KPIs`
- **Format**: `R$ #,##0.00`
- **Description**: Evaluates the net merchandise revenue (item selling price) across all successfully delivered orders, excluding freight charges.

#### 2. Total Delivered Freight Value
```dax
Total Delivered Freight Value = 
CALCULATE(
    SUM(fact_order_items[freight_value]),
    dim_orders[order_status] = "delivered"
)
```
- **Folder**: `01 Financial KPIs`
- **Format**: `R$ #,##0.00`
- **Description**: Total shipping charges billed to customers across delivered orders.

#### 3. Gross Merchandise Value (GMV)
```dax
Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) = 
[Total Delivered Merchandise Revenue] + [Total Delivered Freight Value]
```
- **Folder**: `01 Financial KPIs`
- **Format**: `R$ #,##0.00`
- **Description**: Comprehensive platform gross checkout throughput combining merchandise price and logistics freight.

#### 4. Average Order Value (AOV)
```dax
Average Order Value (AOV) = 
DIVIDE(
    [Total Delivered Merchandise Revenue],
    [Total Delivered Orders],
    0
)
```
- **Folder**: `01 Financial KPIs`
- **Format**: `R$ #,##0.00`
- **Description**: Average merchandise spend per delivered order (excluding freight). Benchmark is R$ 137.04.

#### 5. Estimated COGS (BRL) [Assumed]
```dax
Estimated COGS (BRL) [Assumed] = 
CALCULATE(
    SUM(fact_order_items[assumed_cogs_brl]),
    dim_orders[order_status] = "delivered"
)
```
- **Folder**: `01 Financial KPIs`
- **Format**: `R$ #,##0.00`
- **Description**: Cost of Goods Sold derived from benchmark macro category margins in `config/margin_assumptions.yaml`. Labelled *"assumed margin, not real data"*.

#### 6. Estimated Gross Profit (BRL) [Assumed]
```dax
Estimated Gross Profit (BRL) [Assumed] = 
CALCULATE(
    SUM(fact_order_items[assumed_gross_profit_brl]),
    dim_orders[order_status] = "delivered"
)
```
- **Folder**: `01 Financial KPIs`
- **Format**: `R$ #,##0.00`
- **Description**: Gross profit contribution generated by merchandise sales, calculated using assumed category margin rates.

#### 7. Blended Gross Margin % [Assumed]
```dax
Blended Gross Margin % [Assumed] = 
DIVIDE(
    [Estimated Gross Profit (BRL) [Assumed]],
    [Total Delivered Merchandise Revenue],
    0
)
```
- **Folder**: `01 Financial KPIs`
- **Format**: `0.00%`
- **Description**: Platform-wide blended gross margin percentage. Reconciles to 40.76% across the entire historical catalog.

#### 8. Total Payment Transaction Value
```dax
Total Payment Transaction Value = 
SUM(fact_order_payments[payment_value])
```
- **Folder**: `01 Financial KPIs`
- **Format**: `R$ #,##0.00`
- **Description**: Sum of all monetary settlements captured across credit cards, boletos, vouchers, and debit cards.

### Folder 02: Volume & Operational KPIs

#### 9. Total Orders
```dax
Total Orders = 
DISTINCTCOUNT(dim_orders[order_id])
```
- **Folder**: `02 Volume & Operational KPIs`
- **Format**: `#,##0`
- **Description**: Total count of all orders placed on the platform across all lifecycle statuses (99,441).

#### 10. Total Delivered Orders
```dax
Total Delivered Orders = 
CALCULATE(
    DISTINCTCOUNT(dim_orders[order_id]),
    dim_orders[order_status] = "delivered"
)
```
- **Folder**: `02 Volume & Operational KPIs`
- **Format**: `#,##0`
- **Description**: Distinct count of successfully delivered customer orders (96,478).

#### 11. Total Items Sold
```dax
Total Items Sold = 
CALCULATE(
    COUNTROWS(fact_order_items),
    dim_orders[order_status] = "delivered"
)
```
- **Folder**: `02 Volume & Operational KPIs`
- **Format**: `#,##0`
- **Description**: Total discrete line items fulfilled across delivered orders (110,197 items).

#### 12. Units per Order (Basket Size)

Units per Order = 
DIVIDE(

- **Format**: `0.00`

- **Folder**: `02 Volume & Operational KPIs`

#### 15. On-Time Delivery Rate %

#### 16. Carrier SLA Breach Rate %

    0

#### 17. Average Delivery Lead Time (Days)
```dax
Average Delivery Lead Time (Days) = 
AVERAGEX(
    FILTER(dim_orders, dim_orders[order_status] = "delivered" && NOT(ISBLANK(dim_orders[delivery_lead_time_days]))),
    dim_orders[delivery_lead_time_days]
)
```
- **Folder**: `02 Volume & Operational KPIs`
- **Format**: `0.0 days`
- **Description**: Average calendar days elapsed from initial customer purchase to final delivery doorstep arrival (~12.5 days).

#### 18. Average Carrier Transit Time (Days)
```dax
Average Carrier Transit Time (Days) = 
AVERAGEX(
    FILTER(dim_orders, dim_orders[order_status] = "delivered" && NOT(ISBLANK(dim_orders[carrier_transit_time_days]))),
    dim_orders[carrier_transit_time_days]
)
```
- **Folder**: `02 Volume & Operational KPIs`
- **Format**: `0.0 days`
- **Description**: Average transit duration between seller carrier handover and customer doorstep arrival (~9.3 days).

#### 19. Average Delivery Delay (Late Orders Only)
```dax
Average Delivery Delay (Late Orders Only) = 
AVERAGEX(
    FILTER(
        dim_orders,

    dim_orders[delivery_delay_days]
)
```
- **Folder**: `02 Volume & Operational KPIs`
- **Format**: `0.0 days`
- **Description**: Average calendar delay for orders that breached their SLA (~11.6 days late).

#### 20. Average Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)
```dax
Average Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) = 
CALCULATE(
    AVERAGE(fact_order_reviews[review_score]),
    dim_orders[order_status] = "delivered"
)
```
- **Folder**: `02 Volume & Operational KPIs`
- **Format**: `0.00`
- **Description**: Average survey star rating (1 to 5) for delivered orders (~4.15 overall; 4.29 on-time vs 2.27 late).

#### 21. Detractor Share % (1-2 Stars)
```dax
Detractor Share % (1-2 Stars) = 
VAR TotalReviews = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(fact_order_reviews), dim_orders[order_status] = "delivered")
VAR Detractors = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(fact_order_reviews), dim_orders[order_status] = "delivered", fact_order_reviews[review_score] <= 2)
RETURN
DIVIDE(Detractors, TotalReviews, 0)
```
- **Folder**: `02 Volume & Operational KPIs`
- **Format**: `0.00%`
- **Description**: Proportion of negative customer ratings (1 or 2 stars).

#### 22. Promoter Share % (5 Stars)
```dax
Promoter Share % (5 Stars) = 
VAR TotalReviews = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(fact_order_reviews), dim_orders[order_status] = "delivered")
VAR Promoters = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(fact_order_reviews), dim_orders[order_status] = "delivered", fact_order_reviews[review_score] = 5)
RETURN
DIVIDE(Promoters, TotalReviews, 0)
```
- **Folder**: `02 Volume & Operational KPIs`
- **Format**: `0.00%`
- **Description**: Proportion of perfect 5-star customer ratings.

### Folder 04: Time Intelligence

#### 30. Previous Month Revenue (BRL)
```dax
Previous Month Revenue (BRL) = 
CALCULATE(
    [Total Delivered Merchandise Revenue],
    DATEADD(dim_calendar[Date], -1, MONTH)
)
```
- **Folder**: `04 Time Intelligence`
- **Format**: `R$ #,##0.00`
- **Description**: Evaluates delivered merchandise revenue in the immediately preceding calendar month.

#### 31. Revenue MoM Growth (BRL)
```dax
Revenue MoM Growth (BRL) = 
VAR CurrentRev = [Total Delivered Merchandise Revenue]
VAR PrevRev = [Previous Month Revenue (BRL)]
RETURN
IF(
    ISBLANK(PrevRev),
    BLANK(),
    CurrentRev - PrevRev
)
```
- **Folder**: `04 Time Intelligence`
- **Format**: `R$ #,##0.00`
- **Description**: Absolute month-over-month increase or decrease in delivered merchandise revenue.

#### 32. Revenue MoM Growth %
```dax
Revenue MoM Growth % = 
VAR CurrentRev = [Total Delivered Merchandise Revenue]
VAR PrevRev = [Previous Month Revenue (BRL)]
RETURN
DIVIDE(CurrentRev - PrevRev, PrevRev, BLANK())
```
- **Folder**: `04 Time Intelligence`
- **Format**: `0.00%`
- **Description**: Relative percentage expansion or contraction in monthly merchandise revenue.

#### 33. YTD Delivered Revenue (BRL)
```dax
YTD Delivered Revenue (BRL) = 
TOTALYTD(
    [Total Delivered Merchandise Revenue],
    dim_calendar[Date]
)
```
- **Folder**: `04 Time Intelligence`
- **Format**: `R$ #,##0.00`
- **Description**: Cumulative Year-to-Date merchandise revenue within the calendar year.

#### 34. 3-Month Rolling Average Revenue (BRL)
```dax
3-Month Rolling Average Revenue (BRL) = 
AVERAGEX(
    DATESINPERIOD(
        dim_calendar[Date],
        LASTDATE(dim_calendar[Date]),
        -3,
        MONTH
    ),
    [Total Delivered Merchandise Revenue]
)
```
- **Folder**: `04 Time Intelligence`
- **Format**: `R$ #,##0.00`
- **Description**: Smoothed 3-month rolling average merchandise revenue to dampen weekly fluctuations.
